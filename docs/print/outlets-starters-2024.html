The Fourth Estate · 2024 · Outlets and starters

94 cards: the eight outlets, then the starters for a table of 5, the Editorials and the Scandals. Print on US letter at 100% scale: nine cards a page, cut on the dashed lines.

Outlets · 8 cards · one per player
Your outlet
NewsNation
Ability
Crossover
Your Republican and Democratic currency both count toward either candidate.

Your outlet
The Associated Press
Ability
Race Calls
At the end of the game, +1 wealth for each large state you bought, win or lose.
The AP has declared the winners of American elections since 1848; newsrooms everywhere wait for its call.
Your outlet
The New York Times
Ability
Paper of Record
You never take Scandals.
The Gray Lady passed 10 million subscribers in 2023. Its front page is still where a story becomes official.
Your outlet
The Wall Street Journal
Ability
Business Desk
The first and second Economic stories you play each turn give +1 neutral each.
Published by Dow Jones since 1889, the paper markets read first: prices, rates and the cost of everything.
Your outlet
Politico
Ability
The Scoop
Once a turn, pay 2 neutral: take the topmost card of the main deck. It joins your hand after you draw at clean-up.
Founded in 2007 to cover Washington faster than anyone. Its Playbook newsletter sets the capital's morning agenda.
Your outlet
The New York Post
Ability
Tabloid
Once a turn, discard a Scandal from your hand to draw 3 cards.
Founded by Alexander Hamilton in 1801, now the country's best-known tabloid. A scandal is tomorrow's front page.
Your outlet
Fox News
Ability
Prime Time
Once a turn, when you play your second Social story, draw a card.
The most-watched cable news network of 2024. Its audience turns a moment into a movement.
Your outlet
CNN
Ability
Debate Stage
+1 neutral for each different story you use on its bottom side this turn.
CNN hosted the June debate in Atlanta that reshaped the race, and put both sides on its panels every night.
